Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about FocusBro and troubleshooting tips.

Installation & Setup

Why can't I install the APK?

Make sure you have enabled "Unknown Sources" in your device settings. Go to Settings > Security > Unknown Sources and enable it. Some newer Android versions require you to enable this per-app basis.

What Android version is required?

FocusBro requires Android 5.0 (API level 21) or higher. We recommend having at least 2GB of RAM and 150MB of free storage space for optimal performance.

Is FocusBro available on Google Play Store?

Currently, FocusBro is only available as a direct APK download. We are working on getting it published on Google Play Store and other app stores in the future.

Features & Usage

How do I create my first task?

Tap the floating action button (+) on the main screen, fill in the task details including title, description, priority, and category. You can also set a due date and estimated focus sessions needed.

Can I customize focus session durations?

Yes! You can set custom durations from 1 to 60 minutes. Go to Focus Screen, tap the duration setting, and either use the preset options or set a custom time using the slider or text input.

How do I add background music to focus sessions?

Tap the music icon in the Focus Screen to access the music library. You can choose from built-in ambient sounds or import your own music files. Create playlists and set them to play automatically during focus sessions.

Can I export my notes?

Yes, you can export individual notes or all notes. Tap the share icon on any note to export it as text or share it directly to other apps. You can also backup all your notes from the settings.

Troubleshooting

The app crashes when I try to open a PDF

This might be due to a corrupted or very large PDF file. Try opening a different PDF first. If the issue persists, restart the app and ensure you have enough free storage space. Large PDFs (>50MB) might cause performance issues on older devices.

Focus mode notifications are not working

Check if FocusBro has notification permissions enabled in your device settings. Go to Settings > Apps > FocusBro > Permissions and ensure notifications are allowed. Also check if Do Not Disturb mode is affecting the notifications.

My tasks are not syncing between screens

FocusBro uses local database storage, so tasks should sync automatically between screens. If you're experiencing sync issues, try closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, you may need to restart your device or reinstall the app (make sure to backup your data first).

Data & Privacy

Is my data stored securely?

Yes, all your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite database. FocusBro does not send your personal data to external servers. You can also enable app lock with biometric authentication for additional security.

Can I backup my data?

Yes, you can create backups of your tasks, notes, and settings from the Settings screen. The backup file can be saved to your device storage or shared to cloud storage services. You can also restore from backup when needed.

Does FocusBro collect analytics data?

FocusBro has optional analytics features that you can control from the Privacy settings. All analytics are anonymized and used only to improve the app. You can disable analytics collection at any time without affecting app functionality.
