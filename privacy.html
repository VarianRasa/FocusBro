Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how FocusBro protects your personal information.

Last updated: December 2024
TL;DR: FocusBro stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect, store, or share your personal information with third parties. Your tasks, notes, and usage data remain private and under your control.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Data

FocusBro is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect any personal information such as:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Biometric data or authentication information

1.2 App Usage Data

All data created within FocusBro is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Tasks, notes, and PDF annotations you create
  • Focus session statistics and productivity metrics
  • App settings and preferences
  • Imported music files and playlists

1.3 Optional Analytics

FocusBro includes optional, anonymized analytics that you can control:

  • App crash reports (no personal data included)
  • Feature usage statistics (aggregated and anonymized)
  • Performance metrics for app improvement

Note: All analytics are opt-in and can be disabled at any time in the app settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Local Data Processing

Your data is processed locally on your device to:

  • Provide core app functionality (task management, focus sessions, notes)
  • Generate productivity statistics and insights
  • Sync data between app screens and features
  • Create backups for data recovery

2.2 Analytics Usage

If you opt-in to analytics, anonymized data may be used to:

  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Understand which features are most valuable to users
  • Identify and fix bugs or issues
  • Plan future feature development

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your personal data is stored locally on your device using:

  • Encrypted SQLite database for tasks and notes
  • Secure file storage for imported documents and media
  • Protected app preferences and settings

3.2 Data Security

We implement several security measures:

  • Data encryption at rest using Android's built-in security
  • Optional app lock with biometric authentication
  • No data transmission to external servers
  • Regular security updates and patches

3.3 Backup and Export

You have full control over your data:

  • Create local backups of all your data
  • Export tasks and notes in standard formats
  • Share data with cloud storage services of your choice
  • Delete all data permanently when uninstalling

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 No Third-Party Data Sharing

FocusBro does not share your data with third-party services, including:

  • Analytics companies (unless you opt-in to anonymous analytics)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Cloud storage providers (unless you manually export data)

4.2 Optional Integrations

Some features may optionally integrate with system services:

  • File picker for importing documents and media
  • Share functionality for exporting notes
  • Notification system for reminders
  • Biometric authentication for app security

These integrations are controlled by you and follow Android's permission system.

5. Your Rights and Controls

5.1 Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Modify: Edit or delete any tasks, notes, or settings
  • Export: Export your data in standard formats
  • Delete: Permanently remove all data

5.2 Privacy Settings

Customize your privacy preferences:

  • Enable or disable optional analytics
  • Control app lock and authentication settings
  • Manage notification preferences
  • Configure backup and export options

6. Children's Privacy

FocusBro is suitable for users of all ages. Since we don't collect personal information, there are no special considerations for children's data. However, we recommend parental guidance for users under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we do:

  • We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Significant changes will be highlighted in app update notes
  • Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

8. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data:

  • Review our FAQ section for common questions
  • Check our User Guide for data management instructions
  • Contact us through the app's feedback feature
Remember: Your privacy is our priority. FocusBro is designed to keep your data private and secure on your device. You maintain full control over your information at all times.
